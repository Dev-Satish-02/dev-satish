# The product mindset behind great stories

Stories and products both need a clear user journey, a strong hook, and measurable momentum.

![Book concept](./cover.svg)

## Why this matters

A great story carries the reader through a sequence of decisions, just like a product roadmap guides a user through features.

### Story as a feature map

![](./chart.svg)

## Takeaway

If you think about books like products, you begin to notice the same design patterns: clear goals, feedback loops, and gradual progression.
